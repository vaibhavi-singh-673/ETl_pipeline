Business reports

These procedures return live results from the BigQuery warehouse.

Sales metrics

Monthly revenue, gross sales, discounts, tax, transaction count, month-over-month change, and year-over-year change.

Returns analysis

Category-level sold quantity, returned quantity, return rate, and processed refund impact.
